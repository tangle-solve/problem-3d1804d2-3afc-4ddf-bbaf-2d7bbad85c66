### ALGO-000059: Fourier entropy–influence conjecture
- **Subfield:** Pseudorandomness, derandomization, and Boolean analysis
- **Open:** Prove that every Boolean function satisfies \(H(\widehat f^2)\le C\,\mathrm{Inf}(f)\) for a universal constant \(C\).
- **Status:** Proposed by Friedgut and Kalai in 1996. Many special classes are understood; the universal inequality remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would tightly connect spectral complexity, sensitivity to noise, and learnability.
- **Key works:** Friedgut and Kalai (1996), conjecture; Kalai (2007), *The Entropy/Influence Conjecture*, author exposition.
- **Related:** Probability—noise sensitivity; statistical mechanics—Boolean observables near phase transitions.
- **Aliases:** FEI conjecture.
